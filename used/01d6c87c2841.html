2024 Toyota Tundra SR 4x4 – Rugged Capability and Proven Reliability

Get the job done with absolute confidence in this tough-as-nails 2024 Toyota Tundra SR 4x4. Engineered for maximum durability, efficiency, and real-world utility, the SR trim delivers the legendary, rock-solid reliability Toyota is famous for without any unnecessary fluff. It is the ultimate everyday workhorse or weekend adventure partner.

Under the hood lies a powerful 3.4L Twin-Turbo i-FORCE V6 engine delivering a muscular 358 horsepower and 406 lb-ft of torque, paired seamlessly with a smooth 10-speed automatic transmission. Built on the same high-strength fully boxed steel frame as the top-tier trims, this Tundra gives you exceptional towing capability, highly responsive handling, and excellent fuel efficiency.

Modern Convenience: Enjoy absolute convenience with standard proximity Smart Key entry, push-button start, and automatic climate control.

Toyota Safety Sense 2.5: Packed with premium, standard driver-assist technology including Pre-Collision System with Pedestrian Detection, Full-Speed Dynamic Radar Cruise Control, and Lane Departure Alert with Steering Assist.

Easy Maintenance Cabin: Designed with premium fabric seating and tough, easy-to-clean materials throughout the spacious cabin, perfect for work gear or active families.
