2022 Toyota Tundra Platinum CrewMax 4x4 – Premium Power and Ultimate Luxury

Experience the perfect combination of rugged capability and first-class luxury with this stunning, completely redesigned 2022 Toyota Tundra Platinum CrewMax 4x4. Built on a high-strength steel frame for maximum rigidity and ride comfort, this premium full-size truck delivers an upscale driving experience that sets a new benchmark for the segment.

Under the hood, the advanced 3.5L Twin-Turbo i-FORCE V6 engine produces a commanding 389 horsepower and 479 lb-ft of torque, paired seamlessly with a responsive 10-speed automatic transmission. Whether you're towing a trailer or tackling the daily commute, you'll enjoy impressive performance and significantly improved fuel efficiency over the previous generation.

Platinum Trim Highlights Include:

First-Class Cabin: Relax in premium black semi-aniline leather upholstery, featuring heated and ventilated seats for both front and rear passengers.

Next-Gen Multimedia: A massive 14-inch high-definition touchscreen display takes center stage, complete with wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto compatibility.

JBL Premium Audio: Enjoy concert-quality sound through an immersive 12-speaker JBL premium audio system.

Panoramic Moonroof: Floods the incredibly spacious CrewMax cabin with natural light, offering expansive views for everyone on board.

Advanced Driver Tech: Equipped with a Head-Up Display (HUD), a digital rear-view mirror, and a 360-degree Panoramic View Monitor for effortless parking and trailer hitching.

Toyota Safety Sense 2.5: Drive with total peace of mind thanks to standard cutting-edge safety tech, including Full-Speed Dynamic Radar Cruise Control, Lane Tracing Assist, and Blind Spot Monitoring.
